## Long description (Google Play / App Store)

```
🌊 The tide is rising. You push it back.

Contramarea is a merge puzzle with a twist that changes everything: every move raises the water, and every merge pushes it back. It is not enough to merge fast — you have to decide when to merge to keep your reef above the surface.

Swipe to slide and combine matching pieces, growing them from a tiny Sprout all the way to a Golden Reef. But watch out: when the tide peaks, a new row of water floods in and everything shifts up. When the water reaches the top, the run is over.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🧩 HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe to slide every piece in that direction.
• Two matching pieces merge into the next tier.
• Every move raises the tide; every merge pushes it back.
• Long chains are your best defence — merge several pieces in a single move!

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

✨ WHY IT HOOKS
• A living tide: watch the water rise and fall in real time. Tension without a timer.
• Satisfying chains: the sound and the glow grow with every merge.
• 24 levels with 4 objective types: reach a tier, chain merges, score points, or survive the tide.
• Open Sea mode: endless play to practise or beat your record.
• Powers that change the run: Current, Low Tide and Anchor.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🧘 RELAXING, BUT WITH STAKES
No rush, no punishing timers, no losing to a mis-tap. The pressure comes from the water, not the clock. Play for three minutes or an hour — you can always pick up where you left off.

🎨 ORIGINAL DESIGN
A bioluminescent reef, pieces drawn in code and sound generated in real time. A world built from scratch.

📶 PLAY OFFLINE
Play anywhere, no connection needed. Your progress is saved on your device.

🌍 AVAILABLE IN SPANISH AND ENGLISH

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Contramarea is free. It includes ads and optional in-app purchases. You can remove the ads with a one-time purchase.

Think you can hold back the tide?

Developed by Jessica Panini Cuenca.
```
